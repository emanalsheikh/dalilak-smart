Creamy Vegetable Pasta

Everything you need to prepare a comforting homemade pasta dish.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup cooking cream
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, and herbs

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until tender.
  2. Heat olive oil and cook the garlic and vegetables.
  3. Add the cream, seasoning, and cooked pasta.
  4. Mix everything together and serve warm.
